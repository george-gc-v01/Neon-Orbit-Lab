## 4. Potentially distinctive combination

The potentially distinctive element is the coordinated implementation, interaction and presentation of:

- a statistical Neon high, Neon low and physical-input-priority Neon wow state engine;
- truncated-normal scheduled periods with recorded bounds and distribution parameters;
- reproducible seeded experiments combined with rare, logged secure-random perturbations;
- DPI-correct conversion of a physical 20 mm orbit across monitor scaling configurations;
- immediate genuine physical-input priority and a statistically sampled recovery period;
- a transparent, low-resource Neon low state;
- optional, local experimental logs and end-of-session statistical summaries;
- an accelerated seconds-based Test Mode separated from production timings;
- documented Windows Awake integration with separate system-awake and display-awake controls;
- strong privacy boundaries and an identifiable, non-deceptive process; and
- a coherent minimal neon visual identity and accessibility treatment.

This combination may be distinctive as a product design and body of authored software. Distinctiveness does not automatically mean legal novelty, inventiveness or patentability.

## 5. Elements that are not new by themselves

- Moving a pointer periodically or in a circle.
- Preventing Windows sleep through documented power-management APIs.
- Alternating active and passive periods.
- Normal and truncated-normal probability distributions.
- Pseudorandom seeds, secure random-number generators and rejection sampling.
- Tray applications, countdowns, local logs, reports and themes.
- Pausing automation in response to genuine user input.

These are established ideas. Copyright protects the original expression of source code, documentation and artwork, not the abstract functionality or mathematical method by itself.

## 6. Relationship to established applications

Neon Orbit should be independently engineered and should not copy third-party code, artwork or branding from Mouse Jiggler, Move Mouse, Microsoft PowerToys Awake or Caffeine. It aims to differ through its integrated statistical experiment engine, DPI-correct physical orbit, Neon wow priority, hybrid reproducibility, local reports, accelerated testing, privacy constraints and polished minimal interface.

Claims that it is universally “better” should be avoided until comparative tests support specific statements. The engineering target is to be more integrated, inspectable and experimentally useful in its chosen scope—not to duplicate every feature of the four applications.

## 7. Patent and disclosure caution

Normal distributions and their stated parameters are not patentably novel by themselves. A patent would ordinarily require a new, non-obvious and patent-eligible technical contribution. If patent investigation is serious:

1. Keep the repository private until professional advice is obtained.
2. Do not publicly disclose detailed potentially inventive mechanisms before advice or filing.
3. Preserve dated design notes, commits, test evidence and contributor records.
4. Commission a prior-art search and consult a qualified UK or European patent attorney.

Selecting GPL-3.0 does not preserve exclusivity once the covered software is distributed, and it does not undo a public disclosure.
